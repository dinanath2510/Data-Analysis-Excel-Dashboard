# FNP-Sales-Data-Analysis (Interactive Dashboard Creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
To build an interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes FNP sales data and provides insights into revenue, orders, customer spending, delivery time, product and category performance, occasions, cities, and monthly sales trends for data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset used 
- <a href= "https://github.com/Ayushi0214/FNP---Excel-Project">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- Total Revenue: Identify the overall revenue
- Average Order and Delivery Time: Evaluate the time taken for orders to be delivered.
- Monthly Sales Performance: Examine how sales fluctuate across the months of 2023
- Top Products by Revenue: Determine which products are the top revenue generators
- Customer Spending Analysls: Understand how much customers are spending on Average
- Sales Performance by Top 5 Product: Track the sales performance of too 5 products
- Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders: Find out which cities are placing the highest number of Order
- Quantity vs. Delivery Time: Analyse if higher order quantities impact delivery Times
- Revenue Comparison Between Occasions: Compare revenue generated across different occasions
- Product Popularity by Occasion: Identify which products are most popular during
specific occasions

- Dashboard Interaction <a href="https://github.com/dinanath2510/Data-Analysis-Excel-Dashboard/blob/main/sales%20excel%20projject%20screenshot.png">View Dashboard</a>

## Process
- Data Collection & Cleaning – Cleaned and standardized the FNP sales data using Excel and Power Query.
- Data Analysis – Used PivotTables and formulas to analyze sales, orders, revenue, and customer trends.
- Dashboard Creation – Created interactive charts, KPIs, and filters to visualize key insights.
- Insights & Reporting – Analyzed product, category, occasion, city, and monthly performance for business insights.
